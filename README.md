# Route to Citizenship

A UK immigration **route planner** MVP. Enter the visa you hold today and get:

- a timeline toward **Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR)** and **British citizenship**
- **alternative visa routes** you might switch onto
- basic checks for **residence, absences, English, and the Life in the UK test**
- a **personalised document checklist**
- **reminders** for visa expiry, the ILR window, and tests (in-app, calendar export, optional browser notifications)

Profiles are stored in **browser local storage** only. Nothing is submitted to a backend.

## This is not immigration advice

The app is a planning aid. It is **not** immigration advice, legal advice, or a Home Office service. Rules change. Confirm everything on [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/browse/visas-immigration) or with an OISC-regulated adviser or solicitor before you apply.
